# Changelog

What's new in Session Controller — newest first. Surfaced in-app under **What's new**.
Each entry is tagged with the build it shipped in (the number in the version stamp).

## Build 132 — 2026-09-03
- The menu-bar menu is now minimal — just **Restart** and **Quit** on right-click (so Quit is always one click away). Its old controls moved into the app under **Settings → App**.
- The app keeps the dashboard **always up** while running: it restarts the server automatically if it crashes or wedges.

## Build 131 — 2026-09-03
- Header icons now show a small **MCDU-style tooltip** on hover telling you what each one is.

## Build 130 — 2026-09-03
- **What's new** panel — this changelog, opened from the header, and shown once automatically after an update.
- Tidied the header: the board/list switch and notification toggle moved into **Settings**.

## Build 129 — 2026-09-03
- Dormant sessions now go cold after **24 hours** of no activity (was 2 hours).

## Build 128 — 2026-09-03
- New **overlay**: a slim rail of your In-flight and Holding sessions docked to the right screen edge. Only the colour-coded spines show until you hover one — then that strip flies in so you can read it. It floats over everything, across Spaces. Turn it on from the menu → **Show Overlay**.

## Build 122 — 2026-08-28
- The strip detail view is now a **ring binder** that folds open when you click a strip, and folds shut when you close it.
- **Dev servers** in the detail view are clickable links now — up to three shown at once.
- Parked and Landed strips show their correct labels and colours in the detail view.

## Build 120 — 2026-08-27
- The board **auto-reloads after an update**, so an open window always ends up on the latest version.
- **Help** now explains the MIA states (MIA / Dormant / Wrapped up / Unknown), and how strips drop off (5 days) or are kept for good (add a note).

## Build 116 — 2026-08-26
- A subtle **hover highlight** on strips you can click to open.
- **Buy me a coffee** ☕ in the header.
